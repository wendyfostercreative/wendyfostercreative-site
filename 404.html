See the seriesWriting

I have always been driven by two passions: visual art and creative writing. For years, I saw them as separate forms of creativity. But after meeting artists who painted and worked with clay, and writers who created advertising copy and poetry, I realized that my writing and art are one creative practice expressed in two forms.

I am a writer and I am an artist and like a good relationship, the two endeavors feed and nurture each other giving one space when they need it and balance the strength of the other.
